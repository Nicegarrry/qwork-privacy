# qWork: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-11
**Applies to:** the qWork apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac (`com.qubitstudio.qwork`), including TestFlight builds, and the qWork web app at qwork.qubitstudio.app.

---

## The short version

qWork is a collaborative editor for documents and slide decks, made by Qubit Studio. To work with other people, your account and the files you create are stored on qWork's servers. We use that data only to run qWork for you. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and the apps contain no tracking or advertising SDKs.

Dictation in the app runs on your device. Crash and performance reports stay on your device unless you choose to attach them to a feedback email.

Qubit Studio, Sydney, Australia.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Where it comes from | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| Account details: name, email address, profile picture | You, when you sign up or sign in (with email, Google or Sign in with Apple) | Signing you in, showing your name on comments and shares |
| Your content: documents, decks, folders, comments, review requests, sharing settings | You, as you use qWork | Storing, syncing and sharing your work with the people you choose |
| Push notification token | Your device, if you allow notifications | Telling you about new comments, mentions, shares and finished reviews |
| Basic service logs: request times, errors | Our servers, as you use qWork | Keeping qWork running and fixing problems |

We do not collect your contacts, location, health data, advertising identifier or browsing history.

## On your device only

- **Offline copies.** The app keeps recent files and unsent comments on your device so you can read and comment offline. They are deleted when you sign out.
- **Dictation.** When you dictate a comment, speech is turned into text on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, stored or sent to us.
- **Drawings.** Apple Pencil drawings become part of a comment only when you send it.
- **Crash and performance reports.** iOS and macOS hand the app technical reports through Apple's MetricKit. They contain no content of yours. They leave your device only if you attach them to a feedback email yourself.

## Who processes data for us

To run qWork we use these service providers, each only for the purpose shown:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Clerk | Sign-in and account management |

| Vercel | Hosting the web app |

Your files are visible to the people you share them with, at the level you choose.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and content while your account exists. You can delete files at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email hello@qubitstudio.app from your account email and we will do it within 30 days.
